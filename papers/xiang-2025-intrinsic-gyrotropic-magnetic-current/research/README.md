# Research extensions, separate from the Xiang et al. PRL reproduction

This directory contains follow-up research questions motivated by the IGMC reproduction. None of its results are Xiang et al.'s original outputs, and none may be promoted to a successful material prediction without the stated independent physical gates.

- [BSF × IGMC eight-band microscopic-hopping study](bsf-igmc/): toy bilayer with geometry-generated interlayer tunneling; a model-level demonstration of contrasting unitary/antiunitary switching relations. **No stable sliding ferroelectric state or real-material DFT claim.**

All historical `src/igmc_lab/`, `results/frozen/`, `REPORT_ROUND*.md`, and the `.github/workflows/igmc.yml` paper-reproduction gate are untouched. The research study has its own Python package, frozen data and independent CI.
