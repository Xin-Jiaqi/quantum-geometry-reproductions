# Spin QG × magnetic stacking: peer-work audit (2026-10-21)

**Nature:** independent proposal/prior-art assessment, not a reproduction of Xiang–Jin–Wang (PRL 135, 146303, 2025), not a priority or publishability certification.  
**Scope and source method:** journal landing pages, publisher abstracts, available original-text pages and selected open-access full texts, checked on 2026-10-21. DOI/publishing information is confirmed where linked. For many papers, the exact supplemental equations were **not** checked; distinguish abstract-level overlap from proven equation-level equivalence. Research notes and our numerical examples are **independent model results**, not original-author results.

## 1. How to judge novelty (three different steps)

1. **Idea:** formulate a specific *operator, external field, physical configuration, physical observable*, not just a name such as hidden spin quantum geometry.
2. **Test:** enforce full magnetic symmetry, genuine structural registry and Bloch orbital embedding, degeneracy-covariant spectral projectors, and continuity/contact terms; run controls that eliminate familiar mechanisms.
3. **Peer work:** compare *exact response rank, operator definition, order in E/B, metallic/insulating limit, frequency, scattering protocol and symmetry* to the closest published work. Shared vocabulary is not enough to establish redundancy; conversely nonzero model numbers are not evidence of originality.

Separate three kinds of statement throughout:

- **Already established:** explicit result or claim supported by the linked paper.
- **Our deduction:** inferred overlap / methodological warning; subject to direct equation-by-equation inspection.
- **Not yet established:** remaining hypothesis, physical material realization or observable.

**Corrections against premature earlier brainstorming assertions:**

- A nonzero `Re Tr(P_- J_stag P_+ v_x)/Delta^2` in a PT model is merely a **spectral interband product**, not proof of a positive-definite quantum metric, a DC conductivity or a new spin Hall effect.
- `PT` and `U(2)` projector consistency are necessary mathematical checks, not originality checks.
- A `C_{2z}`-related registry `delta <-> -delta` is **not** automatically a ferroelectric switch (out-of-plane polarization is unchanged under `C_{2z}`).
- A complex/intralayer hopping coefficient inserted as `gamma(delta)` is not a microscopic derivation from real layer registry. If nonzero response survives `t_perp = 0`, the claimed *interlayer* mechanism is not supported.
- In a true insulator, occupation-derivative/Drude terms vanish at zero temperature even if interband kernels stay nonzero; finite T in-gap activated tails are **not** an intrinsic insulator Drude response.
- Novel Bianchi identities or magnetic point-group Neumann constraints alone are typically identities/applications of pre-existing geometry/symmetry.

## 4. Scientific tests needed before any novelty claim

**Gate G1, physical geometry and structure:** construct `t_{alpha beta}(R+delta)` from explicit real-space bonds and *orbital embedding*; verify periodic/Bloch sewing and pair-related configuration `H(k,-delta)=U_g H(g^{-1}k,delta)U_g^{-1}`. Test whether connection reverses a physical ferroelectric polarization; do not assume so.

**Gate G2, PT degenerate electronic structure:** use rank-2 spectral projectors and explicitly check invariance under arbitrary `U(2)` rotations of each doublet. Track that PT is a *physical* magnetic symmetry with the correct representation in an embedded Bloch basis.

**Gate G3, physical spin observable:** define the layer projectors `P_T,P_B`, density `S_T-S_B`, conventional layer spin-current operator, interlayer and local spin torque. Verify `dot S_- = T_inter + T_local`. For finite-q/edge transport include spin-flow divergence and torque-dipole issues; avoid identifying any single coefficient with measured spin current without a boundary or probe calculation.

**Gate G4, contribution and no-go controls:** compare full `t_perp=0` limit, real-only versus spinful/complex tunneling, `delta=0,+delta,-delta`, magnetic order reversal, frequency/disorder/temperature, insulator and metal. Split intrinsic full response from ordinary Fermi-surface relaxation if claimed; no silent insertion of an `alpha(delta)`/ `gamma(delta)` parameter without its actual origin.

**Gate G5, head-to-head novelty:** reproduce closest published *equation-level* definitions (P02/P03/P05/P06/P08/P09/P10/P11/P12/P13/P17). If no independent operator-measurement relation remains after those comparisons, archive as a useful negative result.

## 5. Research priority and practical next action

Keep **B+D** as an integrated *test*, not a claimed new effect. Start with simple 2D square-lattice pair under `C_{2z}`, replacing guessed interlayer phases by distance-dependent real-space hoppings. Derive magnetic symmetry and a layer-staggered response, run `t_perp = 0` and insulator checks. If a response appears only by inserting a registry-dependent *intralayer* odd-dispersion term, label it an **artificial model channel**, and do not promote it as an interlayer geometric discovery.

The precise first-round numerical test and its limitations are documented separately in [STACKING_REGISTRY_GATE1_20261021.md](STACKING_REGISTRY_GATE1_20261021.md) with a script. Its switch operation is **not** shown to be ferroelectric, and the quantity is a **diagonal RTA Fermi-surface coefficient**, not the complete Xiang spin QGT intrinsic/AC result.

### 2026-10-21 Gate 1b amendment: limited four-band operator basis

For the specific one-orbital-per-layer model with `PT=tau_x(i sigma_y)K`, exhaustive 16-matrix enumeration yields only `I, tau_x, tau_y, tau_z sigma_{x,y,z}` as PT-even. Therefore **PT-even direct interlayer spinful hopping cannot be added in this same four-band representation**, even if it looks microscopically appealing. An eight-band model with actual orbital parity/transformations, or a justified departure from PT, must precede the next spinful-hopping test. [Run the check](../scripts/pt_operator_basis_gate.py). The result is mathematically exact **within this representation**, not a new published physics claim.


## New peer-work update, B+D Gate 3 (2026-10-21)

**Additional direct papers to check before any novelty claim:**

- **P18:** Pan, Jiang & Fabian, [*Multistate manipulation of charge-spin conversion in two-dimensional ferroelectric bilayers*, PRB 114, 115135 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1103/np2g-86h3). Confirmed simultaneous FE multistate control of the Edelstein and spin Hall effects. This blocks the generic FE-controlled spin accumulation/switching title or device claim.
- **P19:** Peng et al., [*Sliding Ferroelectricity Driven Spin-Layertronics in Altermagnetic Multilayers*, *Advanced Science* e76050 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.76050). Full text open access. Real CuF2 bilayer FE pairs and layer-locked altermagnetic spin transport already predicted; extremely close in *materials goal*, although the exact atomic-SOC/Kubo operator need equation-level comparison.
- **P20:** Hanke et al., [*Mixed Weyl semimetals and low-dissipation magnetization control in insulators by spin-orbit torques*, *Nature Communications* (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-017-01138-7). Spin-geometry / magnetization parameter mixed curvature and insulator field-induced torque already predate our toy static mixed Berry result.
- **P21:** [*Quantized piezospintronic effect in antiferromagnetic Moiré systems*, *npj Spintronics* (2026)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44306-026-00135-1). Explicit mixed momentum–strain non-Abelian-projector Berry geometry and spin-resolved response. Do not claim novelty for a mere stacking-coordinate/spin-Berry mixed-curvature pump.

**New hard symmetry constraint:** A state preserving *exact* \(PT\) has zero net electric polarization: \(PT:\mathbf P\mapsto-\mathbf P\). A **switchable nonzero \(P_z\)** therefore requires PT breaking in each FE daughter, even if a nonpolar PT parent exists. \(C_{2z}\) does **not** reverse \(P_z\); our old \(C_{2z}\)-paired displacements were not FE switching. A viable magnetic FE *connection* might be \(m_zT\), mapping two distinct states rather than stabilizing either, but must be realized by actual geometry and energy.

**Gate3 study:** [full atomic \(s,p_x,p_y,p_z\) microscopic, Kubo, mixed-curvature and polar-template audit](BD_GATE3_ATOMIC_SOC_MAGNETIC_NO_GO_20261021.md). The full 16-band analysis finds that a \(PT\)-compatible spinful interlayer amplitude can be generated by onsite atomic SOC plus spinless interlayer bonds; however the original layer-opposite AC spin response survives *without magnetism*. The separately magnetic-odd static total-spin \(E\)-response matches standard mixed \((k,b_z)\) Berry curvature to finite-difference accuracy. Therefore neither is yet an independently novel quantum-geometric effect.

**Status:** Gate M model-PASS; magnetic-required claim NO-GO; magnetic-odd static geometry established prior art; polar daughter switching only an external-potential symmetry template. No material-specific or priority assertions.


## New highest-priority competition and Gate 4 cross-check (2026-10-21)

**P22 — closest structural/magnetic connection competitor:** [*A unified symmetry framework for spin–ferroelectric coupling in altermagnetic multiferroics*, *Nature Communications* (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-69635-2), open full text [PMC13039994](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13039994/). Classifies **three** FE switching/spin-band coupling types (spin/FE decoupled; pseudo-time-reversal; nonsymmetric momentum mapping) and DFT-studies actual **six FE minima and multiple paths in MnPS3 bilayers**. Our switching-operator even/odd tensor pair is **not new general magnetic stacking classification**. What remains to compare is a concrete *causal, layer-resolved spin-density/torque spectrum* at the same physical switching paths, not just eigenvalue/spin-label maps.

**P23 — polar registry and topology prior art:** [Bennett et al., *Polar meron-antimeron networks in strained and twisted bilayers*, *Nature Communications* 14, 1629 (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-37337-8), and [Yasuda et al., *Stacking-engineered ferroelectricity in bilayer boron nitride*, *Science* 372, 1458 (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abd3230). AB/BA-polar local minima, electric charge transfer, and a registry-dependent energy landscape in *nonmagnetic* honeycomb materials are already established. Our minimal structural toy reproduces the **symmetry pattern**, not unpublished quantitative polarization.

**P24 — existing FE-driven optical magnetization:** [*Photo-magnetization in two-dimensional sliding ferroelectrics*, *npj 2D Materials and Applications* (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41699-022-00297-6). Light-induced spin/orbital moments, incident-light polarization dependence, and sign control by sliding FE were predicted. A generic **circular-light** spin-\(z\) signal is NOT an untouched loophole to our \(C_3\)-forbidden linear \(E_{\parallel}\to S_z\).

**P25 — additional multiferroic optic close neighbor:** [Chen et al., *Unconventional Magnetism, Sliding Ferroelectricity, and Magneto-Optical Kerr Effect in Multiferroic Bilayers*, *ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces* 17, 69856–69865 (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.5c19598). FE reversal of spin polarization, ferrovalley signal and Kerr response already demonstrated in model+DFT. Do not claim a generic FE switchable magneto-optical readout is new.

**Gate 4 physically resolved toy comparison:** [report](BD_GATE4_POLAR_HONEYCOMB_MAGNETIC_RESPONSE_20261021.md), [four-band structural script](../scripts/bd_gate4_polar_honeycomb.py), [32-band atomic-SOC script](../scripts/bd_gate4b_atomic_spin.py). Main physics boundary: A/B-decorated honeycomb model shows FE-like charge-transfer \(\pm P_z\) and paired **local** stacking minima; with hypothetical A-type AFM and atomic SOC, the two states have identical allowed **total in-plane** spin magnetoelectric susceptibility and opposite **layer-staggered in-plane** susceptibility. However \(C_{3z}\) at the polar local minima **forbids all linear \(E_{\parallel}\to S_z\)**, regardless of the spin QG kernel. A simple parameterized model with no material-specific ionic relaxation does not establish a real magnetic sliding FE.

**Verdict:** the model passes the geometry/symmetry/negative-control gates, but the symmetry classification, static magnetoelectric tensor and FE switching readout have substantial established prior art. The next missing step is an *observable-specific, frequency-resolved, experimentally meaningful interlayer-irreducible physics difference*, verified against P09/P10/P11/P22/P24/P25 before any originality claim.
