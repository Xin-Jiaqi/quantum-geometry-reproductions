At all three slide midpoints the top-metal fraction is respectively \((1/2,1/2),(0,1/2),(1/2,0)\), and the **whole atomic structure** admits
\[
\boxed{\{m_z\mid {\bf t}_{\frac12}\},\qquad
2{\bf t}_{\frac12}\in\Lambda,\quad {\bf t}_{\frac12}\notin\Lambda.}
\]
This is generally a **glide**, not a pure \(m_z\). The translation has no phase for a spatially uniform \(q=0\) bulk-in-plane response. For \(M_{ij}^{(\pm)}=\partial_{B_z}\chi_{S_i^{(\pm)},E_j}\), \(i,j=x,y\), and two **axial** indices (spin, Zeeman) plus one **polar** E index, under horizontal glide:
\[
 M^{(+)}\mapsto -M^{(+)}, \qquad M^{(-)}\mapsto +M^{(-)}.
\]
Here \(S_i^{(+)}=S_i^\mathrm{top}+S_i^\mathrm{bottom}\) and \(S_i^{(-)}=S_i^\mathrm{top}-S_i^\mathrm{bottom}\), with **physically defined layer-local spin projectors**. Thus
\[
\boxed{M^{(+)}(s=1/2)=0,\quad M^{(-)}(s=1/2)\ \text{need not vanish}.}
\]
Another surviving vertical mirror constrains \(M^{(-)}\) to 2 independent diagonal entries in the mirror eigenbasis; it does NOT itself force zero.

The source atomic motif also proves, on every arm and for all \(s\) tested,
\[
\boxed{E(s)=E(1-s),\quad
M^{(+)}(s)=-M^{(+)}(1-s),\quad
M^{(-)}(s)=+M^{(-)}(1-s)}
\]
**provided the relaxed geometry/field/substrate conditions preserve the paired layer-exchange symmetry**. In particular the *total* static NMEE of AB/BA is opposite, but their *staggered* static responses are equal in aligned laboratory axes. A symmetric midpoint is stationary along such a path, but **its barrier, saddle nature, or even metastability cannot be inferred from group theory**. AA from source row 2688 is a different high-symmetry point and should not be assumed to lie on the shortest arm's midposition.

This qualifies the earlier Stages 6–7f: scalar-cubic versus linear-nematic can occur away from the C3v endpoints, but **along these high-symmetry slide arms the intermediate Cs symmetry reduces the full total tensor from the general C1 four entries to two, and the midpoint glide makes it identically zero**. Breaking glide by a substrate, out-of-plane electric field, heterolayer or asymmetric distortion lifts the no-go.

At its own \(u=0\), the independent synthetic eight-band Kane–Mele homobilayer obeys the *pure* horizontal layer-exchange mirror \(U_{m_z}=\tau_x\otimes(I_{\rm sub}\otimes\sigma_z)\): \(UHU^\dagger=H\), \(Uv_xU^\dagger=v_x\), \(US_x^{(+)}U^\dagger=-S_x^{(+)}\), and \(US_x^{(-)}U^\dagger=S_x^{(-)}\). \[
\Gamma^{(+)}_{xz;x}\approx+8.89\times10^{-19},\qquad
\Gamma^{(-)}_{xz;x}\approx-0.0192800323
\]
(original toy-model units). Convergence: 14×48 gives −0.0192797902; 20×60 −0.0192800312; 26×72 −0.0192800323. Varying finite-Zeeman step 0.125–0.5 T retains the sign and near-identical magnitude, with sampled insulating gap distance about 12.6–12.9 meV. **This existence proof is at the toy model's own \(m_z\)-symmetric reference, NOT at the real WSe2 glide midpoint.** It cannot establish a real-material response magnitude.
