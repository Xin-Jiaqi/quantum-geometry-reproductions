## Scientific status and next decision

- **Validated within synthetic gapped T=0 static protocol:** source-independent occupied-bundle mixed Berry curvature, tensor/symmetry identities and channel-resolved numerical benchmarks with explicit assumptions.
- **Not established:** real WSe2 quantitative static NMEE, physical layer-staggered spin coefficient, ferroic switching NEB/stability, SOC spinor operator provenance, or driven-DC transport equivalence.
- **Real material status:** User's local harness completed three genuine Japan IMR WSe2 fixed-xy/z-only ionic calculations, later independently audited original remote raw files and preserved the s=.5 glide; sanitized evidence is committed. **This is neither full ionic relaxation nor quantitative NMEE.** Next priority is a small (explicitly approved) spinor-SOC gap/spin-mixing feasibility check, with separate meV-scale PES convergence before making barrier claims.
- **Reproducibility:** every new stage adds its own `docs/STAGE*`, module, regression tests and machine-readable evidence. The paper's frozen sources and tests remain unchanged. GitHub CI success is a software/model gate, not acceptance of all physical claims.
