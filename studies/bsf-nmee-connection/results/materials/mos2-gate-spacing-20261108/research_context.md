# Physical scope and interpretation

The five user questions are part of this calculation, not a request to expand the model.

1. A twelve-state fit is not required. Retain it only if a small number of states yields a transferable sign criterion while retaining the relevant spin and electric vertices. Existing projection failures must remain visible in the research record. The present full-KS material calculation answers the control question without downfolding.
2. The physical controls are not three equivalent hopping knobs. Vertical field changes screened intralayer potentials, layer detuning and wavefunctions even at fixed ionic structure; this can modify effective hybridization. Separation changes hopping and charge redistribution. Registry changes orbital overlap and sometimes symmetry. External field can additionally move domain walls or relax ions. Therefore clamped-ion fixed-registry results must precede any claim about an actual switching trajectory. A negative field is selected here because the isolated MoS2 layer acquires the opposite sign to the lower-layer interface response.
3. KM remains a transparent conceptual existence proof for same-layer reversal versus cancellation between oppositely responding layers. It should not be presented as reproducing the same dominant channel as MoSSe/MoS2 when the channel tests disagree. A focused main text is justified; concealing contrary controls is not.
4. Common materials are a strength when they establish generality and testability. A new composition is worth adding only if it addresses a concrete gap: stronger signal, accessible reversal threshold, a new symmetry-enforced response or a credible layer-sensitive probe. Material novelty alone cannot repair the missing causal prediction.
5. Spatial selection rules were independently evaluated using the user's toolkit project-pinned v0.18.0 commit 551a0daf (also cross-checked against the older catalog). Spin and magnetic field are axial, electric field polar. No interchange symmetry between E and B is assumed. In C3v, in-plane E with Bz gives Cxx=Cyy and Cxy=Cyx=0, separately in each layer. In D3h the whole-monolayer channel is forbidden. A layer-exchanging inversion or horizontal mirror permits hidden opposite layer responses but forbids their net for this component. A mirror-related pair of complete structures/environments obeys C_lower^B(F)=-C_upper^A(-F); a compensation zero, if it exists, is paired at opposite field. The relation does not force any zero to exist. Lower C2v symmetry permits different x and y diagonal responses, giving a targeted route for later symmetry generalization rather than an immediate 80-layer-group catalogue.

## Verified external context, not evidence of our NMEE

- Yang et al., Nature Communications 15, 1389 (2024), “Non-volatile electrical polarization switching via domain wall release in 3R-MoS2 bilayer”, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-45709-x . Primary abstract/full-page material establishes field-driven domain-wall motion and polarization switching. Thus a fixed-stacking gate sweep is experimentally conditional on domain pinning/tracking. No switching threshold is transferred to our ideal slab.
- Klein et al., Physical Review Materials 6, 024002 (2022), “Electrical control of orbital and vibrational interlayer coupling in bi- and trilayer 2H-MoS2”, https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevMaterials.6.024002 . The primary abstract identifies gate-dependent vibrational and orbital coupling, including delocalized Q-region wavefunctions. This supports the coupling of controls, not our particular NMEE mechanism or a quantitative field threshold in 3R.

## Conditions for a useful negative result

If all sampled lower-layer responses remain negative while the total changes sign, report gate-controlled compensation, not a local spin reversal. If no root is reached by +0.30 A at -0.50 V/nm, report only absence in this tested neighborhood; neither extrapolate an impossible field nor declare all realistic controls ruled out. The BiDB distance is a structural reference, not a newly optimized force-zero distance in this electronic recipe. An increased spacing is not positive compression and must not be called an experimentally applied pressure without a force/energy relation.

## Why the controls can move ions

At leading order, the field-dependent energy of a cell contains -p_z(d, tau) F_z. Because the dipole depends on spacing d and lateral registry tau, a vertical field can change forces in both directions. Electronic detuning at clamped ions and a field-driven change of structure are distinct response paths, even though both are possible in an actual device. No relaxation or switching barrier has been evaluated in this round. A later validation should check only the relevant control points, not launch a broad energy-surface campaign before the electronic prediction is worth testing.

## Closely related general symmetry work checked this round

Li, He, Zhou, Pan, Tan and Sun, “Electrically reversible layer-tensor responses in bilayers”, Physical Review B 114, 295402, published 5 November 2026, https://doi.org/10.1103/2zmv-g9md . The publisher abstract was checked: it generalizes field-odd net tensor responses selected by layer-exchanging stacking operations, with examples including Hall, shift current, nonlinear Drude, piezoelectric, spin Hall and spin photocurrent. Thus the generic symmetry argument for gate-selected layer tensors is prior art; it remains useful for designing our NMEE tests and does not by itself show that their work contains our specific NMEE mechanism. This round did not newly read that paper's full text or SM. The current 3R A structure itself lacks the layer-exchanging m001 symmetry: m001 maps A to a different domain B. Hence C_A(F) need not be odd about F=0; the relevant relation is between A and B at opposite field. A finite compensation offset requires microscopic response information, not symmetry alone.
