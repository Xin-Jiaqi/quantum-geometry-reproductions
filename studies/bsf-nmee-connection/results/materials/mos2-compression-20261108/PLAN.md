# Before-result plan: compression and compensation

User authorizes small out-of-plane compressions of the existing neutral insulating MoS2 3R A registry, including an electrostatic reference. Keep the in-plane cell, relative registry and each monolayer geometry fixed. Two initial increments are -0.10 and -0.20 Angstrom relative to the BiDB reference, roughly3.3% and6.6% of the3.038 A S/S gap. They are geometrical compressions, not pressures already calibrated in GPa or fully relaxed stable paths.

Initial four new points: each compression at Fz=0 and -0.50 V/nm. Reuse d0,Fz=0/-0.50 references. Then select a small number of intermediate gate points near the apparent net zero to test curvature and establish the ordering of compensation fields; do not identify a root solely by a two-point straight line. If the root moves outside the tested bounded field range, report a bound rather than force a reversal. Keep lower and upper responses separate and require an insulating gap.

Prediction written before results: compression may move the A-domain compensation field to a more negative value while keeping lower negative and upper positive. This extrapolates the previous expansion trend; it is not a symmetry theorem. No new B-domain DFT is requested. In a mirror-related complete environment its compensation field would be opposite, conditionally on a zero existing.

Electrostatic reference: extract each layer's smooth plane-averaged ionic+Hartree environment from the same self-consistent field/geometry and apply it to the existing isolated-monolayer full-KS model, without explicit cross-layer electronic states. Keep screening, use two spatial tapers and several retained-state cutoffs; sum the two whole-monolayer responses. This potential already includes the charge redistribution of the full bilayer, and omits XC/nonlocal and in-plane local-field structure. Thus it tests whether smooth electrostatics can reproduce the pressure trend, not whether all microscopic consequences of hybridization have been removed. Matching trends refute uniqueness of this trend as a hybridization fingerprint; mismatch alone does not prove a unique hopping mechanism.

If needed, inspect the existing selected d+p cross-layer hopping intervention in the full KS space as an additional control. It is not the same as the smooth electrostatic reference; do not add channel responses.

References use self-consistent VASP gate fields and true zero B. Probe NMEE remains the existing frozen-potential, spin-only full-KS derivative, checked at96/112 states and b=0.002/0.001 eV.
