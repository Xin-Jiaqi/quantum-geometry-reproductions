# InSe electrical-control comparison: caption and QA

**Caption.** (a) Gate-induced change of the net NMEE coefficient in the InSe
bilayer, two matched isolated layers, and the specified smooth electrostatic
reference. (b) Changes of the lower and upper layer coefficients and one matched
isolated layer. In both panels, delta C means C(F)-C(0): passing through the
origin does not indicate a reversal of the actual spin response. The actual
bilayer remains lower-negative, upper-positive throughout +/-0.10 V/nm.
(c) Signed layer gate sensitivities G=dC/dF, normalized by the sensitivity of
the matched isolated pair for each material. The isolated-pair sensitivity is
negative; positive bars reinforce its direction of gate-induced change,
negative bars oppose it, and black diamonds show their sum. Thus similar net
gains in MoSSe and InSe conceal very different layer-resolved changes.
Shading and open points denote alternate band cutoffs/reference tapers, not
statistical confidence intervals. Lines connect the three calculated fields.

The figure uses the 6x6 gate-SCF exploration. The independent 8x8 zero-gate
check is reported separately and cannot establish convergence of the gate
sensitivity. No giant-response or final device-performance claim is made.

Source data: figure_source.csv; numerical records and their processing are
listed in figure_contract.json. The MoSSe comparison is retained in
mosse_comparison.json, with its original result location. There are no random
samples, fitted errors, p-values, or excluded calculated field points.

Python/matplotlib; 183 x 67 mm, Arial labels 8 pt and ticks 7 pt, editable
PDF/SVG text, 600 dpi PNG preview. The final PNG was visually inspected: no
cropped labels or overlapping legends. Automated source checks pass, with one
